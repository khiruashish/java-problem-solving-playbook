Daily temperatures with a monotonic stack of indexes
  1. The stack holds days still waiting for a warmer day, temperatures decreasing upwards.
  2. Day 0 (73°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  3. Day 1 (74°) is warmer than day 0 (73°) → wait 1. Pop them.
  4. Day 1 (74°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  5. Day 2 (75°) is warmer than day 1 (74°) → wait 1. Pop them.
  6. Day 2 (75°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  7. Day 3 (71°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  8. Day 4 (69°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  9. Day 5 (72°) is warmer than day 4 (69°) → wait 1, day 3 (71°) → wait 2. Pop them.
  10. Day 5 (72°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  11. Day 6 (76°) is warmer than day 5 (72°) → wait 1, day 2 (75°) → wait 4. Pop them.
  12. Day 6 (76°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  13. Day 7 (73°) now waits for its own warmer day: push it.
  14. Days 6 and 7 are never resolved, so they stay 0. Each index is pushed and popped once: O(n).
