House robber in O(1) space
  1. For each house: skip it (keep prev1) or rob it (prev2 + x, since the neighbour is off-limits).
  2. House 0 (2): skip → 0, take → 0 + 2 = 2. best = 2 (take).
  3. House 1 (7): skip → 2, take → 0 + 7 = 7. best = 7 (take).
  4. House 2 (9): skip → 7, take → 2 + 9 = 11. best = 11 (take).
  5. House 3 (3): skip → 11, take → 7 + 3 = 10. best = 11 (skip).
  6. House 4 (1): skip → 11, take → 11 + 1 = 12. best = 12 (take).
  7. Answer 12: rob houses 0, 2 and 4 (2 + 9 + 1). O(n) time, O(1) space.
