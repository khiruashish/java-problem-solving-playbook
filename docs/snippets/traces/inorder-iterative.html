In-order traversal with an explicit stack
  1. Left subtree, then the node, then the right subtree, without recursion.
  2. Go left as far as possible, pushing 1, 2, 4.
  3. Pop 4 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  4. Pop 2 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  5. Go left as far as possible, pushing 5.
  6. Pop 5 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  7. Pop 1 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  8. Go left as far as possible, pushing 3.
  9. Pop 3 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  10. Go left as far as possible, pushing 6.
  11. Pop 6 and visit it. Its left side is done.
  12. In-order: 4 2 5 1 3 6. The stack replaces the call stack: O(n) time, O(h) space.
