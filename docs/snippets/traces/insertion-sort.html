Insertion sort on [5, 2, 4, 6, 1, 3]
  1. The left part is always sorted. Take the next element and slide it left into place.
  2. key = a[1] = 2. Sorted prefix: [5].
  3. 5 > 2: shift it right.
  4. Drop 2 at index 0. Prefix 0..1 is sorted.
  5. key = a[2] = 4. Sorted prefix: [2, 5].
  6. 5 > 4: shift it right.
  7. Drop 4 at index 1. Prefix 0..2 is sorted.
  8. key = a[3] = 6. Sorted prefix: [2, 4, 5].
  9. Drop 6 at index 3. Prefix 0..3 is sorted.
  10. key = a[4] = 1. Sorted prefix: [2, 4, 5, 6].
  11. 6 > 1: shift it right.
  12. 5 > 1: shift it right.
  13. 4 > 1: shift it right.
  14. 2 > 1: shift it right.
  15. Drop 1 at index 0. Prefix 0..4 is sorted.
  16. key = a[5] = 3. Sorted prefix: [1, 2, 4, 5, 6].
  17. 6 > 3: shift it right.
  18. 5 > 3: shift it right.
  19. 4 > 3: shift it right.
  20. Drop 3 at index 2. Prefix 0..5 is sorted.
  21. Sorted. O(n²) worst case, but O(n) on nearly sorted input, stable and in place, which is why real sorts use it for small runs.
