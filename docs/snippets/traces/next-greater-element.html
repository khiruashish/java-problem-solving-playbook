Next greater element with a monotonic stack (right to left)
  1. Walk from the right. The stack holds candidates in decreasing order: anything smaller than a newcomer can never be anyone's answer again.
  2. i = 5: the stack is empty, so nothing to the right is bigger: out[5] = −1.
  3. Push 8: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  4. i = 4: the top of the stack is the next greater element: out[4] = 8.
  5. Push 7: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  6. i = 3, a[i] = 25: pop 7, 8 (≤ 25). 25 is closer and at least as big, so they're useless from here on.
  7. i = 3: the stack is empty, so nothing to the right is bigger: out[3] = −1.
  8. Push 25: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  9. i = 2: the top of the stack is the next greater element: out[2] = 25.
  10. Push 2: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  11. i = 1, a[i] = 5: pop 2 (≤ 5). 5 is closer and at least as big, so they're useless from here on.
  12. i = 1: the top of the stack is the next greater element: out[1] = 25.
  13. Push 5: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  14. i = 0: the top of the stack is the next greater element: out[0] = 5.
  15. Push 4: it's a candidate for everything further left.
  16. Done: [5, 25, 25, −1, 8, −1]. Every value is pushed once and popped at most once: O(n).
