  3. a[j] = 2 < 6: swap it into the small side at i = 0, then i++.
  4. a[j] = 9 ≥ 6: leave it on the big side.
  5. a[j] = 4 < 6: swap it into the small side at i = 1, then i++.
  6. a[j] = 3 < 6: swap it into the small side at i = 2, then i++.
