Tower of Hanoi, n = 3: 7 moves
  1. Move 3 disks from A to C. Trust the recursion: move the top 2 out of the way, move the big disk, then put the 2 back on top.
  2. Move 1: disk 1 from A to C.
  3. Move 2: disk 2 from A to B.
  4. Move 3: disk 1 from C to B.
  5. Move 4: disk 3 from A to C.
  6. Move 5: disk 1 from B to A.
  7. Move 6: disk 2 from B to C.
  8. Move 7: disk 1 from A to C.
  9. All 3 disks on C in 7 = 2³ − 1 moves. T(n) = 2T(n−1) + 1, so n disks take 2ⁿ − 1.
