Trapping rain water with two pointers
  1. Water above a bar = min(tallest on its left, tallest on its right) − its height. Move the pointer with the lower wall: its side's max is the binding limit.
  2. h[l] = 0 < h[r] = 1: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (0) decides. +0.
  3. h[l] = 1 ≥ h[r] = 1: rightMax (1) decides for r. +0.
  4. h[l] = 1 < h[r] = 2: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (1) decides. +0.
  5. h[l] = 0 < h[r] = 2: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (1) decides. +1.
  6. h[l] = 2 ≥ h[r] = 2: rightMax (2) decides for r. +0.
  7. h[l] = 2 ≥ h[r] = 1: rightMax (2) decides for r. +1.
  8. h[l] = 2 ≥ h[r] = 2: rightMax (2) decides for r. +0.
  9. h[l] = 2 < h[r] = 3: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (2) decides. +0.
  10. h[l] = 1 < h[r] = 3: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (2) decides. +1.
  11. h[l] = 0 < h[r] = 3: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (2) decides. +2.
  12. h[l] = 1 < h[r] = 3: the right wall is taller, so leftMax (2) decides. +1.
  13. The pointers met: 6 units of water, one pass, O(1) space.
